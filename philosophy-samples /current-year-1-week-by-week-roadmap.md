# Semester 1: Thinking, Reality, and Perception

## Unit 1: What is Thinking? 
**Theme:** Understanding thought, reasoning, and self-awareness.

### 1. What is thought?
* **Read:** *Frederick*
* **Activities:** Thought Experiment: "What if we couldn’t think?"; Test memory vs. reality

### 2. How do we think?
* **Read:** *Philosophy for Kids* (Heraclitus)
* **Activities:** Hypothetical thinking; Debate: "Are dreams a kind of thinking?"; Discuss alternate realities

### 3. Can we think wrong?
* **Read:** *The Emperor’s New Clothes*
* **Activities:** Logical mistakes; Activity: Find fallacies in daily life; Fallacy Spotting Game; Identify logical errors in media

### 4. Recap
* **Activities:** Reflection on “Thinking”; Discussion: "What is the biggest thinking mistake we’ve learned about?"

---

## Unit 2: What is Real? 
**Theme:** Understanding perception, illusions, and reality.

### 5. Seeing vs. Knowing
* **Read:** Plato’s Cave discussion
* **Activities:** Thought Experiment: "If we lived in a VR world, would it be real?"; Optical Illusions & Reality; Compare different perceptions
* **Chinese Idiom Story:** *The Man from Zheng Buys Shoes* (Trusting rigid rules over actual reality)

### 6. What If Reality is Different?
* **Read:** The parable of "The Blind Men and an Elephant"
* **Activities:** Discuss: "Can two people see the same thing differently?"; Shadow & Mirror Experiments; Explore perception shifts
* **Chinese Idiom Story:** *The Frog in the Well* (Having a limited perspective on reality)

### 7. Deception & Illusions
* **Read:** *The Boy Who Cried Wolf*
* **Activities:** When do people believe things too easily?; Lying & Trust Experiment; Test believability of statements
* **Chinese Idiom Story:** *Pointing at a Deer and Calling it a Horse* (Deliberate deception and manipulating reality)

### 8. Recap
* **Activities:** Debate on Reality: "Can we ever be sure what’s real?"; Create an "Illusion Journal"
* **Chinese Idiom Story:** *The Bow's Reflection as a Snake* (False alarms and illusions created by the mind)

---

## Unit 3: What is True? 
**Theme:** Understanding proof, logic, and truth.

### 9. What is Truth?
* **Read:** *The Velveteen Rabbit*
* **Activities:** Proof concepts; Discussion: "What is something we thought was true but wasn’t?"; Two Truths and a Lie
* **Chinese Idiom Story:** *The Shield and the Spear* (Spotting a self-contradiction)

### 10. Can we Trust Facts?
* **Read:** *The Fallacy Detective*, Ch. 3
* **Activities:** Fact vs. Opinion Activity; Fact-checking GPT; Test AI’s knowledge & biases
* **Chinese Idiom Story:** *Suspecting the Neighbor of Stealing the Axe* (Confirmation bias shaping our facts)

### 11. Logic Puzzles & Deduction
* **Read:** An excerpt from a Sherlock Holmes story by Arthur Conan Doyle (e.g., *The Adventure of the Red-Headed League* or *The Adventure of the Blue Carbuncle*)
  * *Alternative:* The story of Oedipus and the Riddle of the Sphinx
  * *Alternative:* A case from the *Encyclopedia Brown* series by Donald J. Sobol
* **Activities:** Knights & Knaves problems; Discussion: "What makes a puzzle different from a trick?"; Spot the Contradiction; Identify logical inconsistencies
* **Chinese Idiom Story:** *Looking at a Leopard Through a Tube* (Deducing the whole truth from limited facts)

### 12. Recap
* **Activities:** Reflect on Learning; Journal: "What is something I once believed but now changed my mind about?"
* **Chinese Idiom Story:** *Drawing Legs on a Snake* (Ruining the truth by adding unnecessary parts)

***

# Semester 2: Logic, Reasoning, and Arguments

## Unit 4: Cause & Effect, Predicting Outcomes 
**Theme:** Understanding patterns, causation, and logical sequences.

### 13. What is Cause & Effect?
* **Read:** *If You Give a Mouse a Cookie*
* **Activities:** Discuss chain reactions; Cause & Effect Puzzles; Find examples in daily life; Story sequencing
* **Chinese Idiom Story:** *Waiting by a Stump for a Hare* (Mistaking random luck for a reliable cause-and-effect pattern)

### 14. Can We Predict the Future?
* **Activities:** Discuss forecasting; Play "What Happens Next?"; Simple Probability Games (Flip coins, roll dice, discuss likelihood)
* **Chinese Idiom Story:** *The Old Man Loses His Horse* (Unpredictable outcomes and the chain reaction of fortune)

### 15. Is Everything Determined?
* **Read:** Aristotle's Four Causes
* **Activities:** Domino Effect Experiment; Observe how small changes affect outcomes
* **Chinese Idiom Story:** *The Foolish Old Man Removes the Mountains* (How small, cumulative causes create massive effects)

### 16. Recap
* **Activities:** Patterns in Life; Journal: "What patterns do I notice in the world?"
* **Chinese Idiom Story:** *The Man of Qi Worries About the Sky* (Anxiety over impossible or highly unlikely outcomes)

---

## Unit 5: What Makes a Good Argument? 
**Theme:** Constructing and evaluating arguments, persuasion.

### 17. What is an Argument?
* **Read:** *The Fallacy Detective*, Ch. 6 (Straw Man Fallacy)
* **Activities:** Discuss persuasion; Spot the Argument; Identify arguments in daily conversations
* **Chinese Idiom Story:** *Three in the Morning, Four in the Evening* (Being fooled by how an argument is framed)

### 18. What Makes an Argument Strong?
* **Read:** Aesop's Fable: "The North Wind and the Sun"
* **Activities:** Learn structure of a logical argument; Practice stating opinions; Debate Club with Toys: Have toys argue different sides of a silly issue
* **Chinese Idiom Story:** *Going South but Driving North* (When actions contradict the stated argument/goal)

### 19. What is a Bad Argument?
* **Read:** Ad Hominem
* **Activities:** Identify weak arguments; Bad Argument Challenge; Try to trick each other with false reasoning
* **Chinese Idiom Story:** *Judging People by Their Appearance* (The Ad Hominem fallacy in action)

### 20. Recap
* **Activities:** Debate: "Should robots have feelings?"; Journal: "What makes a strong argument?"
* **Chinese Idiom Story:** *Discussing Military Tactics on Paper* (The flaw of theoretical arguments that ignore reality)

---

## Unit 6: Thinking Traps & Biases 
**Theme:** Identifying common logical fallacies and cognitive biases.

### 21. What is a Thinking Trap?
* **Read:** *The Fallacy Detective*, Ch. 12 (Special Pleading)
* **Activities:** Discuss biases; Find Thinking Traps in Stories; Identify flawed reasoning in fairy tales
* **Chinese Idiom Story:** *Covering One's Ears to Steal a Bell* (The trap of self-deception)

### 22. Why Do People Believe Wrong Things?
* **Read:** Aesop's Fable: "The Fox and the Grapes"
* **Activities:** Discuss common cognitive biases; Memory vs. Reality Test; Play a memory distortion game
* **Chinese Idiom Story:** *Sour Grapes* (Rationalizing to protect one's ego)

### 23. Can AI Have Biases?
* **Read:** A simplified news article or blog post on a real-world example of AI bias
* **Activities:** Explore how AI models can make mistakes; Test AI Bias: Ask GPT or Claude loaded questions and analyze responses
* **Chinese Idiom Story:** *Blind Men Touching an Elephant* (How bias forms from only having part of the data)

### 24. Recap
* **Activities:** Debate: "Can we trust our own minds?"; Activity: "Recreate a conversation from memory"

***

# Semester 3: Ethics, Fairness, and Moral Choices

## Unit 7: What is Fair? 
**Theme:** Understanding fairness, justice, and equity.

### 25. What is Fairness?
* **Read:** *The Little Red Hen*
* **Activities:** Discuss: "Does fairness mean equal?"; Divide Unequal Treats; Experiment: Share a treat unfairly and discuss reactions

### 26. Is Fairness Always Possible?
* **Read:** *Philosophy for Kids*, Question 18 (Justice and Fairness)
* **Activities:** Fair vs. Unfair Scenarios; Create scenarios and decide if they are fair or unfair

### 27. Game Theory: Sharing & Fairness
* **Read:** A simplified, narrative version of "The Prisoner's Dilemma"
* **Activities:** Play "Ultimatum Game" (negotiating fairness); Fairness in AI: Ask GPT about fairness, discuss AI decision-making

### 28. Recap
* **Activities:** Debate: "Is it ever okay to be unfair?"; Journal: "What does fairness mean to me?"

---

## Unit 8: What is Right & Wrong? 
**Theme:** Understanding morality, ethical dilemmas, and different perspectives on right vs. wrong.

### 29. What is Morality?
* **Read:** *The Tortoise and the Hare*
* **Activities:** Discuss: "Is winning always good?"; Ethical Dilemma Roleplay: Play out different ethical choices and their consequences

### 30. Trolley Problem: Making Choices
* **Read:** "The Lady, or the Tiger?" by Frank R. Stockton
* **Activities:** Introduce Trolley Problem; Discuss: "Would you pull the lever?"; Debate Different Trolley Variations: Adjust the scenario and discuss the impact of changes

### 31. Are Rules Always Right?
* **Read:** *The Boy Who Cried Wolf*
* **Activities:** Discuss: "Are all lies bad?"; Rules Experiment: Create a new rule and test how it affects daily life

### 32. Recap
* **Activities:** What Makes Something "Good" or "Bad"?; Journal: "When was I in an ethical dilemma?"

---

## Unit 9: Responsibility & Consequences 
**Theme:** Exploring the impact of actions, responsibility, and decision-making.

### 33. What is Responsibility?
* **Read:** *The Giving Tree*
* **Activities:** Discuss: "Do we have obligations to others?"; Personal Responsibility List: Create a list of responsibilities at home, discuss their importance

### 34. Do Our Actions Always Have Consequences?
* **Read:** *The Fallacy Detective*, Ch. 15 (Slippery Slope)
* **Activities:** Discuss unintended consequences; Consequence Mapping: Predict consequences of different actions

### 35. AI & Responsibility: Can a Machine be Blamed?
* **Read:** Isaac Asimov's "Three Laws of Robotics" and a summary of his short story "Runaround"
* **Activities:** Explore AI ethics and accountability; Design AI Ethics Rules: Create rules that an AI should follow

### 36. Recap
* **Activities:** Debate: "Who is responsible when technology makes mistakes?"; Activity: "Invent a machine that makes ethical choices"

***

# Semester 4: Abstract Thought, AI, and the Universe

## Unit 10: Time & Change 
**Theme:** Understanding the nature of time, change, and paradoxes.

### 37. What is Time?
* **Read:** *A Wrinkle in Time* (excerpt)
* **Activities:** Discuss: "What would happen if time stopped?"; Time Experiment: Create a time diary and record how time feels different in different activities

### 38. Does the Past Still Exist?
* **Read:** *Philosophy for Kids*, Question 25 (Plato on time)
* **Activities:** Discuss: "If you remember something, does it still exist?"; Time Travel Scenarios: Create different time travel stories and discuss their possibilities

### 39. Time Paradoxes
* **Activities:** Discuss Zeno’s Paradoxes and the Grandfather Paradox; Create a Time Loop Story: Write or draw a short story about a time paradox

### 40. Recap
* **Activities:** Debate: "Would you rather go to the past or the future?"; Journal: "If I could time travel, what would I do?"

---

## Unit 11: AI & Ethics 
**Theme:** Understanding artificial intelligence, machine ethics, and responsibility.

### 41. What is AI?
* **Read:** *Philosophy for Kids*, Question 30 (Machines and Thinking)
* **Activities:** Discuss: "Can a machine think?"; AI Experiment: Ask GPT/Claude to answer a question, then analyze its reasoning

### 42. Can Machines Have Morals?
* **Read:** The Turing Test (simplified)
* **Activities:** Discuss: "If AI learns from humans, can it be moral?"; Create an AI Moral Code: Design ethical rules for an AI to follow

### 43. AI Mistakes & Biases
* **Read:** The story of "The Sorcerer's Apprentice" (from Goethe's poem or Disney's *Fantasia*)
* **Activities:** Explore real-world examples of AI bias and unintended consequences; Test AI Bias: Ask GPT/Claude the same question in different ways and compare responses

### 44. Recap
* **Activities:** Debate: "Should AI be allowed to make important decisions?"; Activity: "Design an AI that makes ethical choices"

---

## Unit 12: What Can We Know? 
**Theme:** Exploring skepticism, infinite knowledge, and the limits of human understanding.

### 45. Can We Ever Know Everything?
* **Read:** Gödel’s Incompleteness Theorem (simplified)
* **Activities:** Discuss: "Are there things we can never understand?"; Knowledge Puzzle: Play a game where one person has secret information and others must figure it out

### 46. Is Seeing Believing?
* **Read:** Plato’s Allegory of the Cave (review)
* **Activities:** Discuss: "What if our senses deceive us?"; Illusion & Reality: Experiment with optical illusions and discuss perception

### 47. What if We Are in a Simulation?
* **Read:** The Simulation Hypothesis (simplified)
* **Activities:** Discuss: "How do we know if we’re real?"; Create a Thought Experiment: Invent a scenario where we might be living in a simulated world

### 48. Recap
* **Activities:** Debate: "If life were a simulation, would it matter?"; Journal: "What is the most mysterious question in the universe?"
