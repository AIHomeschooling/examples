# Current 5-Year Philosophy Roadmap

## Year 1: Foundations of Thinking, Reality, and Ethics
* **Key Topics:** 
  * What is thought? What is real? What is true? 
  * Cause and effect. 
  * Introduction to arguments and basic fallacies. 
  * Subjective vs. objective reality. 
  * Introduction to AI as a thinking tool.
* **Learning Goals:** 
  * Develop metacognitive awareness. 
  * Introduce epistemology through concrete examples. 
  * Build vocabulary for discussing thinking. 
  * Establish that ideas deserve serious examination.

## Year 2: The Architecture of Reasoning & Cooperative Ethics
* **Key Topics:** 
  * Informal logic and argument structure. 
  * Hidden premises and assumptions. 
  * Emotional reasoning and cognitive biases. 
  * Conditional logic (if-then). 
  * Fairness frameworks and distributive justice. 
  * Game theory and cooperation. 
  * Philosophy of language introduction. 
  * AI alignment fundamentals.
* **Learning Goals:** 
  * Strengthen deductive and evaluative reasoning. 
  * Develop intellectual empathy through perspective-taking. 
  * Understand how minds (human and artificial) systematically err. 
  * Build a foundation for formal logic without premature formalization.

## Year 3: Philosophy of Science, Mathematics, and Society
* **Key Topics:** 
  * What makes knowledge scientific? Hypothesis, evidence, falsification. 
  * Philosophy of mathematics: discovery vs. invention. Why does math describe reality? 
  * Social contract theory, justice, and institutions. 
  * Technology ethics and dual-use problems. 
  * Thought experiments as a philosophical method.
* **Learning Goals:** 
  * Develop scientific reasoning and healthy skepticism. 
  * Connect mathematical thinking to philosophical questions. 
  * Understand how societies organize and justify rules. 
  * Evaluate technology's role in human flourishing.

## Year 4: Mind, Consciousness, and Agency
* **Key Topics:** 
  * Philosophy of mind: What is consciousness? The hard problem (age-appropriate). 
  * Personal identity over time. 
  * Free will revisited: compatibilism, moral responsibility. 
  * Decision theory and rational choice. 
  * Value specification and learning. 
  * Advanced AI alignment: corrigibility, goal stability, interpretability.
* **Learning Goals:** 
  * Deep engagement with questions of selfhood and agency. 
  * Connect free will to moral responsibility. 
  * Understand decision-making frameworks. 
  * Develop a sophisticated understanding of alignment challenges.

## Year 5: Metaphysics, Existence, and Future Thinking
* **Key Topics:** 
  * Nature of time and change. 
  * Possibility and necessity (modal reasoning). 
  * Simulation arguments and skeptical scenarios. 
  * Long-term thinking and existential risk. 
  * Futures of intelligence: biological, artificial, hybrid. 
  * Philosophy of probability and uncertainty. 
  * Personal philosophy synthesis.
* **Learning Goals:** 
  * Grapple with fundamental questions of existence. 
  * Develop comfort with deep uncertainty. 
  * Think rigorously about long-term futures. 
  * Integrate learning into a coherent personal philosophical framework.
