# Original 5-Year Roadmap

## Year 1: Thinking, Reality, Ethics, AI Introduction
* **Key Topics:**
  * **Thinking, Reality, and Perception** – How do we think? What is real? What is true?
  * **Logic, Reasoning, and Arguments** – How do we reason? What makes a good argument?
  * **Ethics, Fairness, and Moral Choices** – What is right? What is fair? What are responsibilities?
  * **Abstract Thought, AI, and the Universe** – Big ideas: time, AI, and the limits of knowledge.
* **Learning Goals:**
  * Develop critical thinking and reasoning.
  * Introduce ethics & fairness as foundations for AI alignment.
  * Explore big questions about knowledge, reality, and perception.

## Year 2: Foundations of Formal Logic & Advanced Ethics
* **Key Topics:**
  * **Symbolic Logic & Proofs** (if/then, contradiction, Venn diagrams).
  * **Advanced Fairness Questions** (game theory, distributive justice).
  * **AI & Bias** – How do AIs learn? What mistakes can they make?
* **Learning Goals:** 
  * Strengthen deductive reasoning & ethical problem-solving.

## Year 3: Philosophy of Science & Society
* **Key Topics:**
  * **What is Science?** (What makes a "good" experiment?)
  * **Rules & Laws** – What makes a fair society?
  * **Technology & Ethics** – Can technology be “moral”?
* **Learning Goals:** 
  * Introduce scientific reasoning and political philosophy.

## Year 4: Free Will, Consciousness, and the Mind
* **Key Topics:**
  * **Do we have free will?** (Debate determinism vs. choice).
  * **What is the mind?** (Philosophy of consciousness).
  * **Can AI be conscious?** (AI Alignment, sentience debates).
* **Learning Goals:** 
  * Deep dive into cognition, identity, and consciousness.

## Year 5: Metaphysics & Existential Questions
* **Key Topics:**
  * **What is time?**
  * **Are we living in a simulation?**
  * **The Future of AI & Humanity**
* **Learning Goals:** 
  * Explore the nature of existence, reality, and AI futures.
