# Lesson Samples

These are samples from our AI Homeschooling sample lessons for an early-elementary learner, shared as part of our blog series on my Substack [AI Homeschooling](https://aihomeschooling.substack.com/).

These materials were generated using Claude, Gemini, and ChatGPT, then reviewed and revised by a parent. They reflect where our specific learner was at the time they were created — they are not general-purpose curriculum materials and are not designed to work out of the box for other children.

We share them as documentation of process, not as templates or recommendations. Please use your own judgment.
